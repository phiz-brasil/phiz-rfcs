In short: consent in = policy + trust · consent out = product + enforcement.
Why we must record consent anyway
LGPD (Brazil's data protection law): when consent is the legal basis, the company carries the burden of proving it was obtained correctly, and the user may withdraw it at any time. The data protection authority (ANPD) can investigate and fine.
Other rules: consumer protection (complaints to Procon / consumer bodies) and sector rules for banks and fintechs add their own expectations on customer communication. Messages sent on other legal bases (contract, legitimate interest — e.g. collections) still need that basis documented.
Meta: without a record, the company cannot honor opt-outs reliably, and complaints quickly lower its quality rating.
Minimum record: who · which company · what was authorized (category) · where · when · exact wording · status / revocation date.
Decision for PhizChat
A. Meta model: policy only; businesses keep their own records; platform enforces opt-outs and quality. Less friction, less control.
B. Platform registry: an opt-in endpoint / in-app consent stored by PhizChat and checked before each send. More control and auditability, more integration effort.
