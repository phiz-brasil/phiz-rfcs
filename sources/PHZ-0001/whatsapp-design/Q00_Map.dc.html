WHY Q MATTERS
Most Brazilian companies already collect consent in their own channels (see O1, O2). If PhizChat only accepted in-app consent, a company like PicPay could not start conversations with its existing customers. Flow Q allows that, while the user keeps the same controls as in flow P.
Under the LGPD the business must prove consent either way; model B also lets PHIZ check it before every send.
