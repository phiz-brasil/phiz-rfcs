5 rules before a business can message
Meta WhatsApp Business Platform
RULE ① · OPT-IN
The user must authorize first
The company needs the user's explicit permission to contact them on WhatsApp, naming the company — in its app, a form, a store, or inside WhatsApp (flow O). Messaging without it leads to blocks, reports and restrictions.
② Business-initiated = templates only
Messages the company starts must use a Meta-approved template, in four categories:
Marketing offers, campaigns, launches
Utility payments, orders, bills
Authentication one-time codes
Service replies to user questions
③ 24-hour service window
User's last message → 24h: free replies
Then: templates
Every user message restarts the 24-hour timer.
④ Marketing message limits
Meta caps how many marketing messages a user can receive (exact number not published); extra sends fail.
⑤ Quality rating
Blocks, reports and "not interested" taps — typical when users never opted in — lower the number's quality rating and can limit how much the company may send.
