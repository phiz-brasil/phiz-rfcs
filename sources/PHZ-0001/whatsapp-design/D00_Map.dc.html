Design insight: the user holds many brakes — stop offers, block, report — and each directly affects the company's ability to send. Opt-in and opt-out are two ends of the same consent: whatever the user authorized (flow O) can be withdrawn here at any time. PhizChat should design both sides plus a quality rating.
